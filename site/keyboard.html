DEBUG keyboard

Hands on the keys.

Single letters for what you do all day: E next error, F follow, P freeze, G to the whole log. Every shortcut is an action you can rebind in the app.

INFO questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Log Commander is open source under the MIT license. Use it at home and at work, read the code, change it.

Where can I download it?

Version 1.0.0 is on the GitHub releases page: a universal .dmg for macOS, an installer or .msi for Windows, .deb or AppImage for Linux. You can also build it from source with Node.js and Rust; the steps are in the README.

Are the packages signed?

Not yet. On macOS open the app with right click, Open. On Windows choose More info, Run anyway in SmartScreen.

What happens with my logs?

They stay on your computer. The app has no account, no telemetry and does not phone home. If you turn on AI control, what the AI reads goes to the provider of your AI app, and you can hide tabs from it.

How large can a file be?

Up to 2³² lines per file. The index takes 16 bytes per line, the text stays on disk. A line is shown up to 128 KB and searched up to 1 MB.

Can it sort by a column?

No. The view stays in file order, because a log is a sequence. To see “the slowest requests”, filter on a field or ask the dashboard with SQL.

Which language is the interface?

German for now, as the pictures on this site show.

$ log-commander /var/log/app.log
